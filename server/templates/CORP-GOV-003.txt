## 1. Introduction and Purpose

[ORG_NAME] is committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity and impartiality in all its activities. A conflict of interest arises when an individual's personal interests, or those of a connected person, could actually or potentially influence the exercise of their duties and responsibilities to [ORG_NAME].

This policy sets out the organisation's approach to identifying, disclosing, and managing conflicts of interest. It applies to all members of the [BOARD_NAME], members of the [EXEC_COMMITTEE_NAME], employees at all levels, contractors, consultants, and volunteers acting on behalf of [ORG_NAME].

Failure to declare a conflict of interest is a serious matter that may constitute a breach of contract, a breach of fiduciary duty, or, in certain circumstances, a criminal offence. [ORG_NAME] will take appropriate action in response to any undisclosed conflict.

---

## 2. What Constitutes a Conflict of Interest

A conflict of interest may be actual, potential, or perceived. It is not necessary for a conflict to result in actual impropriety — the appearance of impropriety is itself damaging to the organisation and must be managed.

### 2.1 Examples of Conflicts

The following are indicative examples of situations that may give rise to a conflict of interest:

**Financial Interests:**
- Holding a financial interest (shares, equity, loans, or debt) in a supplier, competitor, or partner organisation
- Receiving personal financial benefit, commission, or remuneration from a third party in connection with your role at [ORG_NAME]
- Having a family member or close associate employed by, or holding a significant interest in, a supplier or partner

**Organisational Involvement:**
- Serving as a director, trustee, or officer of another organisation whose interests may conflict with those of [ORG_NAME]
- Holding a voluntary position or advisory role with a body that competes with or regulates [ORG_NAME]

**Gifts, Hospitality, and Benefits:**
- Accepting gifts, hospitality, or other benefits from suppliers, clients, or third parties that could influence or appear to influence your professional judgement (see also the Code of Conduct, CORP-GOV-005)

**Personal Relationships:**
- Involvement in recruitment, performance management, procurement, or contractual decisions involving a family member, close friend, or romantic partner

This list is illustrative and not exhaustive. Where an individual is uncertain whether a situation constitutes a conflict, they should seek guidance from [CEO_NAME] or contact [COMPLIANCE_EMAIL].

## 3. Disclosure Requirements

### 3.1 Duty to Declare

All individuals within scope of this policy have a continuing duty to disclose any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest. Disclosure must be made:

- At the point of joining [ORG_NAME] (during induction or on appointment to the [BOARD_NAME])
- Promptly on becoming aware of a new or changed interest at any subsequent time
- Prior to any meeting, decision-making process, or activity in which the conflict is relevant

### 3.2 How to Declare

Declarations should be made in writing to [CEO_NAME] using the Conflict of Interest Declaration Form, available from [COMPLIANCE_EMAIL]. Members of the [BOARD_NAME] must also declare relevant interests at the commencement of any [BOARD_NAME] meeting in which a conflicting matter arises.

Where the conflict involves [CEO_NAME], the declaration should be made directly to the Chair of the [BOARD_NAME].

---

## 4. The Conflicts of Interest Register

[ORG_NAME] maintains a Conflicts of Interest Register, overseen by the Company Secretary or designated compliance function. The register records all declared interests and the management measures applied to each.

The register is reviewed:
- At each [BOARD_NAME] meeting
- Annually as part of the policy review cycle

The register is stored at [POLICY_STORAGE_LOCATION] and access is restricted to authorised personnel only. Aggregate or anonymised summaries may be provided to the [BOARD_NAME] for assurance purposes.

---

## 5. Managing Conflicts of Interest

Upon receiving a declaration, [CEO_NAME] (or the Board Chair where the conflict involves [CEO_NAME]) will determine the appropriate management measure. Options include:

**Withdrawal:** The individual withdraws from all discussions, decisions, and access to information relating to the conflict.

**Restriction:** The individual is permitted to participate in discussion but not in the decision or vote.

**Monitoring:** Where the conflict is deemed low risk, it is recorded on the register and monitored without further restriction, with reasons documented.

**Termination:** In the most serious cases, where a conflict cannot be adequately managed, the individual may be required to divest the conflicting interest or, in extreme cases, to resign from their role.

The chosen management measure will be documented in the Conflicts of Interest Register along with the rationale. The individual concerned will be notified in writing.

---

## 6. Gifts and Hospitality

All gifts and hospitality offered to or received by employees and Board members in connection with their role at [ORG_NAME] must be recorded in the Gifts and Hospitality Register. Guidance on acceptable thresholds is set out in the Code of Conduct (CORP-GOV-005). Any gift or hospitality that creates or could create a conflict of interest must be declared under this policy.

---

## 7. Enforcement and Consequences

Any breach of this policy — including failure to declare a conflict, making a false declaration, or acting in a situation of undisclosed conflict — will be treated as a serious disciplinary matter. Depending on the circumstances, consequences may include:

- Formal disciplinary action up to and including dismissal (for employees)
- Removal from the [BOARD_NAME] (for directors or trustees)
- Referral to a regulatory authority or law enforcement agency where a criminal offence may have been committed
- Civil recovery of any financial benefit obtained through an undisclosed conflict

Concerns about potential or undisclosed conflicts should be reported to [CEO_NAME] or to [COMPLIANCE_EMAIL]. Anonymous reports may be made via the whistleblowing mechanism set out in CORP-GOV-004.

---

## 8. Training and Awareness

All new joiners will receive training on this policy as part of their induction. Refresher training will be provided annually and whenever a material update to the policy is made. Completion of training will be recorded by the HR function.
