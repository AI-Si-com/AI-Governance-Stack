## 1. Introduction and Purpose

Effective budget management is a cornerstone of sound financial governance. This policy sets out the framework within which [ORG_NAME] plans, allocates, monitors, and controls its financial resources through the annual budget cycle and beyond.

The budget is the primary tool through which [ORG_NAME] translates its strategic objectives into operational plans, and through which the [BOARD_NAME] holds the executive team accountable for financial performance. Sound budget management enables the organisation to make informed decisions, respond to changing circumstances, and demonstrate responsible stewardship to its stakeholders.

This policy applies to all budget holders, the [EXEC_COMMITTEE_NAME], the [BOARD_NAME], and the finance function led by [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME]. It must be read alongside the Financial Management Policy (CORP-FIN-001), the Delegation of Authority Policy (CORP-GOV-002), and the Procurement & Purchasing Policy (CORP-FIN-003).

## 2. Budget Setting Process

### 2.1 Annual Budget Cycle

The annual budget cycle for [ORG_NAME] follows the timetable below. Specific dates for any given financial year will be communicated by [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] to all budget holders at the start of the process.

| Stage | Lead | Indicative Timing |
|-------|------|------------------|
| Strategic planning guidance issued | [CEO_NAME] | 4 months before year-end |
| Departmental budget submissions | All budget holders | 3 months before year-end |
| Budget consolidation and challenge | [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] | 2.5 months before year-end |
| Draft budget review | [EXEC_COMMITTEE_NAME] | 2 months before year-end |
| Draft budget presented to [BOARD_NAME] | [CEO_NAME] / [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] | 6 weeks before year-end |
| Budget approved | [BOARD_NAME] | Before financial year start |
| Budget communicated to budget holders | [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] | Within 5 days of approval |

### 2.2 Strategic Planning Guidance

Prior to departmental budget preparation, [CEO_NAME] will issue planning guidance setting out:

- Key strategic priorities for the forthcoming year
- Any known significant cost pressures or income assumptions
- The overall financial parameters within which budgets must be prepared (e.g. expected growth or efficiency targets)
- Any new activities or investments to be reflected

### 2.3 Departmental Budget Submissions

Each budget holder is responsible for preparing their departmental budget in the format specified by [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME]. Submissions must:

- Be based on realistic and justifiable assumptions
- Reflect planned activity levels and known cost commitments
- Identify any new investment requirements and provide business case justification
- Be consistent with the strategic planning guidance issued by [CEO_NAME]

Budget holders are encouraged to discuss their submissions with [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] prior to formal submission. Queries should be directed to [FINANCE_EMAIL].

### 2.4 Budget Challenge and Approval

[FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] will consolidate departmental submissions into an organisation-wide draft budget and facilitate a budget challenge process with the [EXEC_COMMITTEE_NAME]. The draft budget will be tested for:

- Affordability and overall financial balance
- Consistency with strategic objectives
- Robustness of underlying assumptions
- Adequacy of contingency provisions

The [EXEC_COMMITTEE_NAME] will review and endorse the draft budget before it is submitted to the [BOARD_NAME] for approval. The [BOARD_NAME] is the sole authority for approving the annual budget.

## 3. Budget Monitoring and Reporting

### 3.1 Monthly Monitoring

[FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] will produce monthly management accounts within 15 working days of each month end. Reports will include:

- Actual income and expenditure versus budget for the month and year-to-date
- Forecast outturn for the full financial year
- Commentary on significant variances
- Narrative on key financial risks and opportunities

Monthly reports are circulated to [CEO_NAME] and the [EXEC_COMMITTEE_NAME]. A summary is presented to the [BOARD_NAME] at each [BOARD_MEETING_FREQUENCY] meeting.

### 3.2 Budget Holder Responsibilities

Each budget holder is responsible for:

- Reviewing their budget report monthly
- Contacting [FINANCE_EMAIL] promptly to investigate and explain any variances
- Forecasting their expected year-end position and updating it as circumstances change
- Raising any anticipated overspend with [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] at the earliest opportunity

Failure to monitor budgets effectively may result in management action and escalation to [CEO_NAME].

---

## 4. Variance Reporting

### 4.1 Materiality Thresholds

Variances must be formally explained by budget holders in the monthly reporting cycle where they exceed the following thresholds:

- **Adverse variance of more than 5% of the annual budget** for the relevant cost or income line, where this exceeds £500
- **Any adverse variance exceeding £5,000**, regardless of percentage
- **Any income shortfall exceeding £2,500**

### 4.2 Corrective Action

Where a significant adverse variance is identified, the budget holder must:

1. Provide a written explanation of the cause to [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] within five working days
2. Confirm whether the variance is expected to self-correct or will persist to year-end
3. Propose corrective action where the overspend is expected to continue

[FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] will escalate material variances to [CEO_NAME] and, where significant, to the [BOARD_NAME].

## 5. Virement Rules

A virement is the transfer of budget from one budget line to another to reflect changed circumstances. Virements do not increase the overall approved budget and do not require [BOARD_NAME] approval, but they must be managed in accordance with the following rules:

| Virement Value | Approver |
|----------------|----------|
| Up to £2,500 | Budget Holder (notified to [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME]) |
| £2,501 to £10,000 | [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] |
| £10,001 to £25,000 | [CEO_NAME] |
| Above £25,000 | [EXEC_COMMITTEE_NAME], reported to [BOARD_NAME] |

Virements that would materially alter the nature of planned activities (e.g. reducing front-line service delivery to fund administrative costs) require approval from [CEO_NAME] and may require [BOARD_NAME] notification.

Budget holders must request virements in writing to [FINANCE_EMAIL]. Retroactive virements will only be granted in exceptional circumstances.

---

## 6. Supplementary Budgets and Unbudgeted Expenditure

Where material expenditure that was not included in the approved budget becomes necessary during the year, a supplementary budget must be sought. Supplementary budgets require:

- A written business case submitted to [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME]
- Approval from [CEO_NAME] for amounts up to £25,000
- Approval from the [BOARD_NAME] for amounts above £25,000

All approved supplementary budgets will be formally incorporated into the revised forecast and reported to the [BOARD_NAME].

---

## 7. Year-End Processes

### 7.1 Commitments and Accruals

At the financial year end, [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] will ensure that all known commitments (goods or services received but not yet invoiced) are captured as accruals in the final accounts. Budget holders must provide their year-end commitments schedule to [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] by the deadline communicated each year.

### 7.2 Underspends and Carry-Forwards

Year-end underspends do not automatically carry forward. Requests to carry forward underspent budget into the new financial year must be submitted to [FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] with a business justification. All carry-forward requests are subject to approval by [CEO_NAME] and are incorporated into the new year budget as a supplementary allocation.

### 7.3 Year-End Close

[FINANCE_LEAD_NAME] is responsible for managing the year-end close process and for the preparation of draft statutory accounts, in accordance with the timeline set out in the Financial Management Policy (CORP-FIN-001).

---

## 8. Policy Compliance

Budget holders who breach this policy — including by incurring expenditure without appropriate authority, failing to monitor their budget, or providing inaccurate forecast information — may be subject to management action. Serious or repeated breaches will be escalated to [CEO_NAME].

Questions regarding budget management should be directed to [FINANCE_EMAIL].
