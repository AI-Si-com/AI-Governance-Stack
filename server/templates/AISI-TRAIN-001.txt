## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes [ORG_NAME]'s requirements for training and awareness programmes relating to artificial intelligence. As AI becomes increasingly embedded in organisational processes and decision-making, it is essential that all employees have the knowledge and skills needed to use AI responsibly, recognise AI-related risks, and fulfil their obligations under [ORG_NAME]'s AI governance framework.

This policy applies to all [EMPLOYEE_COUNT] employees, contractors, and agency workers at [ORG_NAME]. Specific requirements are differentiated by role, with enhanced training obligations for those in departments where AI is actively used: [DEPARTMENTS_LIST]. It applies to new starters, existing staff, and those transitioning into new roles involving AI.

Responsibility for implementing this policy rests with [HR_LEAD_NAME] (HR) and [AI_INCIDENT_LEAD] (AI Incident Lead), with operational coordination through [HR_EMAIL].

## 2. Mandatory AI Literacy Training (All Staff)

All employees must complete a baseline AI literacy training programme within 30 days of joining [ORG_NAME] and annually thereafter. This training is mandatory for all staff regardless of whether they directly use AI tools.

### 2.1 Core AI Literacy Programme — Learning Objectives

By completing the core programme, all employees will be able to:

- Describe what AI and machine learning are in plain terms and how they differ from conventional software
- Identify the AI tools in use at [ORG_NAME] and understand their intended purposes
- Recognise situations where AI outputs should be treated with caution or verified
- Understand [ORG_NAME]'s AI governance policies and their personal responsibilities under them
- Know how to report an AI-related concern or incident
- Understand the basics of data protection as it applies to AI processing, including the use of personal data in AI systems
- Recognise AI-generated content and understand the risks of presenting it without review or disclosure

### 2.2 Core Programme Format and Duration

- Minimum duration: 2 hours (may be delivered as e-learning, instructor-led, or blended)
- Must include a knowledge assessment with a pass mark of at least 80%
- Must be refreshed annually; staff who have not completed the annual refresh may not operate AI tools beyond basic use

## 3. Role-Specific Training

In addition to the core programme, employees in specific roles must complete enhanced training tailored to their responsibilities.

### 3.1 AI Operators and End Users

Employees who use AI tools as part of their day-to-day role within [DEPARTMENTS_LIST] must complete:

- **Tool-specific onboarding training** for each AI system they are authorised to use, covering its purpose, inputs, outputs, limitations, and safe use guidelines
- **Practical exercises** demonstrating appropriate and inappropriate uses of the tool
- **Quality review skills** — how to assess, verify, and where necessary override AI outputs in their domain
- Training specific to their department must be designed and maintained jointly by [HR_LEAD_NAME] and the relevant department head

### 3.2 AI Decision Reviewers

Employees who review or rely on AI outputs to inform significant decisions (see AISI-HUMAN-001) must complete:

- **Automation bias awareness training** — understanding the psychological risks of over-reliance on AI
- **Critical review techniques** — structured methodologies for evaluating AI recommendations independently
- **Domain-specific AI risk training** relevant to their area (e.g. HR AI tools in recruitment, credit models in financial services, clinical AI in health settings)
- Minimum duration: 4 hours, refreshed annually

### 3.3 AI System Owners and Technical Staff

Employees responsible for configuring, maintaining, or overseeing AI systems must complete:

- All core and operator training
- Technical AI governance training covering [ORG_NAME]'s full AISI policy suite
- AI bias detection and testing methodologies (see AISI-BIAS-001)
- Data management and lineage requirements for AI (see AISI-DATA-001 and AISI-DATA-002)
- AI incident identification and response (see AISI-INC-001)
- Minimum duration: 8 hours, refreshed annually, with additional updates when material policy changes occur

### 3.4 AI Governance Leads and Senior Management

[AI_INCIDENT_LEAD], members of the senior leadership team, and those with specific AI governance responsibilities must complete:

- Senior leadership AI governance training covering ethical AI, accountability, regulatory developments, and the organisation's AI risk appetite
- Training on the UK AI governance framework and applicable regulatory expectations
- Minimum duration: 4 hours per year, including at least 2 hours from an external provider to ensure exposure to independent perspectives

## 5. Competency Assessment

Training completion is a necessary but not sufficient measure of competence. [HR_LEAD_NAME] and [AI_INCIDENT_LEAD] must ensure that:

- All mandatory training programmes include a formal assessment at completion
- Pass marks must be achieved before the employee is permitted to proceed to the relevant AI activity
- Employees who fail an assessment must be given one additional attempt following remedial support; persistent failure must be escalated to the relevant line manager and [HR_EMAIL]
- For AI decision reviewers and system owners, competency must be re-assessed annually or following a relevant incident

Competency assessment records must be maintained by [HR_EMAIL] alongside training completion records.

---

## 6. Training Records

[ORG_NAME] is required to maintain accurate training records to demonstrate compliance with this policy and with applicable regulatory expectations. Training records must:

- Record each employee's completion of each mandatory programme, including date, method of delivery, and assessment outcome
- Be maintained for the duration of employment plus three years
- Be accessible to [HR_LEAD_NAME], [AI_INCIDENT_LEAD], and authorised auditors upon request
- Be reported to [CEO_NAME] quarterly, showing completion rates across [DEPARTMENTS_LIST]

Employees who are overdue for mandatory refresher training must be notified by [HR_EMAIL] and given a maximum of 30 days to complete the outstanding training before their access to AI systems is restricted.

---

## 7. Emerging Risks Awareness

The AI landscape evolves rapidly, and new risks — including adversarial attacks, hallucination in large language models, deepfake content, and novel regulatory requirements — emerge regularly. [ORG_NAME] will issue timely awareness communications to all staff as significant new risks are identified.

[AI_INCIDENT_LEAD] is responsible for:

- Monitoring AI risk developments from the AI Safety Institute, ICO, NCSC, and other relevant bodies
- Issuing briefings to all relevant staff within 10 business days of a significant development
- Determining whether emerging risks require an immediate update to training content

[HR_LEAD_NAME] will ensure that awareness communications are distributed effectively across all departments and that receipt is confirmed where appropriate.

---

## 8. Induction for New Starters

All new starters must complete the core AI literacy programme as part of their induction before being granted access to any AI tools. [HR_LEAD_NAME] is responsible for ensuring AI training is embedded in the standard induction programme for all new joiners. Role-specific training must commence within 60 days of the employee taking up their substantive role.

---

## 9. Contractors and Third Parties

Contractors, agency workers, and third parties who access [ORG_NAME]'s AI systems or data must confirm completion of equivalent AI governance training with their own employer, or complete [ORG_NAME]'s core programme, before access is granted. Compliance is the responsibility of the engaging manager, with oversight from [HR_EMAIL].
