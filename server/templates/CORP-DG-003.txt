## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the requirements for retaining, reviewing, and securely disposing of records and personal data held by [DATA_CONTROLLER_NAME]. It applies to all staff, contractors, and third parties who create, store, or manage records in any format (electronic or paper) on behalf of the Organisation.

Retention of data beyond its necessary period increases risk and may constitute a breach of UK GDPR data minimisation and storage limitation principles. Equally, premature destruction of records may breach legal or regulatory obligations. This policy strikes the appropriate balance between these requirements.

## 4. Disposal Methods

Records must be disposed of securely using a method appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

### 4.1 Electronic Records

- Personal data held on IT systems must be permanently deleted using a method that prevents recovery. Simple deletion to the recycle bin is not sufficient.
- Hard drives and portable media being decommissioned must be wiped using [DATA_CONTROLLER_NAME]-approved overwrite software or physically destroyed by an approved third party.
- Cloud-hosted data must be deleted from the service and confirmed as deleted by the provider.
- All electronic disposal must be logged. Contact [IT_EMAIL] to arrange disposal of IT assets.

### 4.2 Paper Records

- Documents containing personal or confidential data must be cross-cut shredded using an approved shredder (minimum DIN 66399 security level P-4) or placed in a designated confidential waste sack for collection and destruction by an approved contractor.
- Bulk disposal of paper records must be authorised by [DPO_NAME] and a destruction certificate obtained from the contractor.
- Plain waste paper recycling must not be used for documents containing any personal data.

## 5. Disposal Logging

All disposal of personal data — whether electronic or paper — must be recorded in the Organisation's Disposal Register, maintained by [DPO_NAME]. Entries must include: date of disposal, description of data, volume, disposal method, and name of authorising person. The Disposal Register is available from [COMPLIANCE_EMAIL].

---

## 6. Legal Hold

Where records may be relevant to litigation, a regulatory investigation, a data subject rights request, or an audit, normal retention and disposal rules are suspended. A legal hold notice will be issued by [DPO_NAME] or the Organisation's legal advisers specifying the records subject to hold and the hold period. Staff must not destroy any records subject to a legal hold, even if the scheduled retention period has expired. Legal hold queries should be directed to [COMPLIANCE_EMAIL].
